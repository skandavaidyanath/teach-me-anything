      - b2 Pillar one: the membership fee: Membership fees of about 65 dollars from over 70 million households are roughly half of Costco's operating profit, so the store can sell goods near cost and still make money.
      - b3 Pillar two: fewer items, faster turns: By stocking about 4,000 items instead of a supermarket's 30,000, Costco buys in huge volume, sells inventory before paying suppliers, and lets suppliers' money fund its shelves.
      - b4 The catch: the renewal rate: The whole model depends on members renewing, and Costco's renewal rate above 90 percent is what makes the fee income reliable enough to keep prices low.
      - b5 Verdict: why cheap prices are the product: Low prices are what Costco sells to keep members paying the fee, so the model works only while trust and renewal stay high, and copying it requires all three pillars together.
